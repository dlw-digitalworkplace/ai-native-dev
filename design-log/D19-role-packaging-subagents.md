# D19 — Role packaging

- **Area:** Role packaging (cross-cutting; config layer)
- **Date:** 2026-06-26
- **Status:** Superseded by D20

## Decision
**(SUPERSEDED by D20, 2026-06-26 — reverted to in-session commands.)** Intake and the planner are re-packaged from in-session slash commands into cold subagents (`agents/intake.md`, `agents/planner.md`), each fronted by a thin command (`/aind:intake`, `/aind:plan`) that does nothing but delegate to its subagent and relay the result.** The subagent owns the full procedure, status transitions, and stuck-state handling; it carries its own `name`, `color`, and — the decisive capability — its own **`model`** (intake and planner may run on different models). The thin command stays the human entry point and the `claude -p` / Actions handle (D6). **This is a packaging change on the *config* side of the D1–D15 line only — the flow is untouched:** the status model (D4), the gates, and the plan-PR mechanics (D5/D10/D17) are unchanged, and signing enforcement is preserved because the `PreToolUse` hook matches the `Bash` tool globally and fires for a subagent's calls exactly as for the session's. **`polish` is explicitly NOT promoted** and stays warm (D7).

## Rationale
The `CLAUDE.md` warm/cold rule conflated two axes: (a) *independence from an upstream agent's hot context* (the real reason the reviewer/test-writer/E2E/dreamer are cold — D7/D8/D9/D16) and (b) *being the human's entry point*. Intake and the planner were classed "warm" only on axis (b); on axis (a) they have **nothing hot to inherit** — intake re-grounds from the story + rubric, the planner from the story + rules + codebase, both already "from artifacts." Their human gate is **asynchronous on an artifact** (intake's signed verdict comment + tag; the planner's resolvable PR threads, D5), not live steering mid-run — the planner is even specified to *never block on a human* (proceed-on-assumption). So their warmth was incidental, unlike `polish`, which is warm for a **real** reason (D7: it deliberately shares the coder's context to avoid paying twice). Three drivers force the move: **(1)** headless/automation — a self-contained ID-in/PR-out subagent is exactly the D6 trajectory; **(2)** redundant warmth — no live steering happens, so in-session buys nothing; **(3)** per-role model selection — a slash command **inherits the session model and cannot override it**, while a subagent carries a `model:` field, so giving intake and the planner different models is *only* possible as subagents. The thin-command-fronts-cold-subagent shape keeps the human entry point and the "commands are thin / mechanics in scripts" invariant intact while isolating each role's context and unlocking the model knob. Keeping `polish` warm preserves the one place warmth is load-bearing.
